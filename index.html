Data, briefly.

The Datalysts publishes short, data-led videos on politics and economics: one claim, one chart, the source on screen, and an honest caveat at the end.

We use recognised sources only, such as Our World in Data, the OECD, the IMF, the World Bank, national statistics offices and peer-reviewed research. On contested questions we show the main finding and the strongest objection.

Find us on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

Datalysts Publisher

Datalysts Publisher is an internal tool that publishes The Datalysts' own videos to The Datalysts' own Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to other users.

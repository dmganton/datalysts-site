Data deletion

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Datalysts Publisher only connects to The Datalysts' own accounts and does not store data about other people.

If you believe we hold data about you and want it deleted, email dimeglio.anton@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We will confirm deletion within 30 days.

To remove the tool's access to an account, revoke it in that platform's settings: Meta (Settings > Apps and websites), TikTok (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions) or Google (myaccount.google.com > Security > Third-party access).
